In 2011, we were asked to contribute to a Sovereign Grace Music project. What came out of it was "Behold Our God," a song that sets the greatness of God in plain words for a whole congregation to sing.

![Jonathan and Ryan](/img/email/brothers.jpg "medium")

We didn't write it to be popular. We wrote it because the truth in it needed a tune plain enough for a whole room to sing at once. It ended up in churches we'll never visit, in languages we don't speak, which is the only kind of success a hymn can really have.

::: verse
Say to the cities of Judah, "Behold your God!"

**Isaiah 40:9**
:::

Most of what we release now follows the same rule: say something true, and make it singable.
