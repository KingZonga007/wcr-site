# Worship Is Warfare: page content (from Ryan, 2026-11-08)

Schedule: live on YouTube, Tuesdays and Thursdays at 6am MST (confirmed 2026-11-08; the "Tue–Fri" wording below is outdated and should read Tuesday and Thursday on the site). Podcast on Apple, Spotify and Amazon Music.

## Description
In addition to their produced music, which is available on all streaming services, WCR also has a daily livestream/podcast (Tue–Fri) called Worship is Warfare. Worship is Warfare seeks to do battle with our hearts over what will control our affections. Who will we serve? Every day that battle rages, and worship is warfare seeks to fix your eyes on Jesus every morning through worship and encouragement in the Word.

## Spurgeon (commentary on Psalm 119)
He who rushes from his bed to his business and waits not to worship is as foolish as though he had not put on his clothes or cleansed his face, and as unwise as though he dashed into battle without arms or armor. Be it ours to bathe in the softly flowing river of communion with God before the heat of the wilderness and the burden of the way begins to oppress us.

So that is what we are seeking to do: to help you strap on your spiritual armor every morning, to put on Christ through worshiping Christ, every day.

## Alternate copy
Our songs and show Worship is Warfare is about lifting our eyes to Christ daily. Every morning the war for allegiance begins. Who will you serve? Will the day be decided by what is screaming the loudest and demanding you give it your attention, or will you be about the business of your King? Worship is spiritual warfare. The fight to make Christ our greatest delight and treasure. Our goal is to help you start your day lifting your eyes above earthly concerns, setting your face to see the glory of God, and putting your first steps of the day on holy ground.
